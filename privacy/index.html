Pixl Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Pixl is a social app for discovering, playing and sharing games, operated by Orthors (“we”, “us”, “our”). Orthors is the data controller for your personal data when you use the Pixl iOS app (the “App”).

Questions about this policy or your data: crew@orthors.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

Information you give us

  • Account information — your email address, username and password (stored by our authentication provider in hashed form). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the identifier and the details they choose to share (such as your name and email, which may be an Apple private relay address).
  • Profile information — your display name, profile photo, bio and any link you choose to add. If you pick a profile photo, the App accesses only the photo you select, or the camera when you choose to take one.
  • Content you create — comments, likes, reposts, saved games, follows, and messages you send in direct and group chats, including games you share in chats.
  • Games you publish — game files, titles, captions, thumbnails and categories if you upload games.
  • Reports and blocks — reports you make about content or users, and accounts you block.
  • Communications — anything you send us directly, such as support emails.

Subscription information

If you subscribe to Pixl Plus, Apple processes your payment. We do not receive your card details or Apple ID. We receive and store subscription records from Apple — the transaction identifier, product, status and expiry date — so we can give you the Plus features.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage information — the games you view, play, like and share, scores and leaderboard entries, play sessions, and your searches. We use this to run the feed and leaderboards and to recommend games.
  • Device and technical information — device type, operating system and app version, your IP address when the App connects to our servers, and a push notification token if you allow notifications. The App also makes a small connectivity check to a Google server to detect whether you are online.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide the App: accounts, the game feed, playing games, social features, chat, leaderboards and uploads;
  • To show your profile and public activity to other users, as the App is designed to work;
  • To send notifications you have allowed (such as messages, likes and comments);
  • To provide and verify Pixl Plus subscriptions;
  • To keep the App safe, including moderating content, handling reports, preventing abuse and enforcing our Terms;
  • To respond to support requests and to meet legal obligations.

Our legal bases under UK GDPR are: performance of a contract (providing the App you signed up for), legitimate interests (keeping the App safe, secure and improving it), consent (for example, push notifications and photo access, which you can withdraw in your device settings), and legal obligation.

4. Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We use service providers to run Pixl, under terms that protect your data:

  • Supabase — our backend provider for accounts, the database, file storage and server functions.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store subscriptions and payment, and push notification delivery.
  • Google — Sign in with Google, if you choose it, and the connectivity check described above.
  • Expo — a push notification delivery service used to send notifications to your device.
  • Hosting and content delivery providers — which host the games and media shown in the App.

Your username, profile photo, published games, comments and likes are visible to other users by design. Messages are visible to the people in the conversation. We may disclose data where required by law or to protect the rights and safety of Pixl, our users or others.

Some providers process data outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, we rely on safeguards such as the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision.

5. Advertising and tracking

This version of the App does not show advertising and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for any permission the law or Apple requires before it takes effect.

6. Games run inside the App

Games are made by third-party creators and run in a web view inside the App. We scan uploaded games and our Terms forbid games that collect personal data without disclosure, but we cannot guarantee how every game behaves. Please report any game you think is behaving badly.

7. Offline saves

If you save games for offline play, the game files are stored on your device. They are removed when you remove a save, sign out, or delete the App.

8. How long we keep your data, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the App under Profile → Settings → Login & security → Delete account. Deleting your account removes your account and the personal data associated with it. We may keep limited records where the law requires, or to resolve disputes and enforce our terms.

Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Cancel Pixl Plus first in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

9. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, correct it, have it erased, restrict or object to certain processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To use these rights, email crew@orthors.co.uk. We will respond within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

10. Children

Pixl is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at crew@orthors.co.uk and we will delete it.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), hashed passwords and access controls. No system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make material changes, we will tell you in the App before they take effect. The “Last updated” date above shows the current version.
