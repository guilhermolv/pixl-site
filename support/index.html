FAQ

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Settings → Login & security → Delete account. This removes your account and its personal data, as described in our Privacy Policy. It does not cancel an App Store subscription — cancel Pixl Plus first (see below).

How do I cancel Pixl Plus?

Open iOS Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Pixl, then choose Cancel Subscription. You keep Plus until the end of the period you’ve paid for. You can also manage it from Profile → Settings → Plans & billing.

I subscribed but Plus isn’t active

Open Profile → Settings → Plans & billing and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in to the same Pixl account you subscribed with. If it still doesn’t work, email us with the email address on your account.

For a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, tap “Forgot password?” and enter your email. We’ll send you a link that opens the app so you can choose a new one.

How do I report a game, message or user?

Use the report option in the app’s menus. You can also block a user from their profile. You can email us with a link or username and we’ll review it promptly.

How do I remove a game I’ve published?

Open the game from your profile and delete it from its options menu. It will be removed from the feed.

I found a bug

Email us with what happened and what device and iOS version you’re on.
